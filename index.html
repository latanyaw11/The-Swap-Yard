⚠️
Barter Reporting Strategy Under IRS rules, the fair market value of goods or services you receive in a barter exchange is taxable income. The Swap Yard tracks your barter FMV automatically.

Your obligations: Report all barter income on Schedule C or Schedule 1 of your tax return. If your total barter FMV for the year exceeds $600, The Swap Yard will issue a 1099-B form.

Best practice: Set FMV on every listing, confirm FMV at time of trade, and keep records of all exchanges. Export your barter log below for your accountant.

The Swap Yard is the eco-conscious alternative to buying new. When you trade, sell, or buy secondhand, you're keeping goods out of landfills and reducing demand for new manufacturing. That's real environmental impact — and it costs nothing extra.

Less Manufacturing Waste

Every secondhand purchase means one fewer item manufactured — reducing the raw materials, energy, and emissions tied to producing something new.

Fewer Landfill Items

The average American generates over 4 pounds of trash per day. Trading goods on The Swap Yard directly diverts usable items from that waste stream.

Community Over Consumption

Barter and trade build real relationships between neighbors. Swapping a guitar amp for a camera lens creates community in a way that clicking "Add to Cart" never can.
