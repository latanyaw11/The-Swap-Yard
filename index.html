Why The Swap Yard

Trade better. Live greener.

♻️
Good for the planet
Every item traded is one fewer thing in a landfill. Swap Yard makes sustainable living practical, not preachy.
💸
No fees on barter
Pure trades cost nothing — zero platform fee. Cash transactions have a small fee that keeps the lights on.
🏡
Rooted in RTP
Built for Durham, Raleigh, Chapel Hill, and Cary first — then nationwide. Real people, real neighbors.
🔒
Safe by design
Verified sellers, public meetup spots, escrow for high-value trades, and handoff codes to confirm every deal.
📱
App on any phone
No App Store needed — install directly from your browser to your home screen in two taps. Always up to date.
🌐
Your rules
You set the price, the payment method, and the terms. We just connect you — the deal is always yours.
