How It Works

Barter Taxes — The Real Story

The IRS considers the Fair Market Value (FMV) of what you receive in a trade to be income. That sounds scary — but here's what it actually means for most Swap Yard traders.

💡

The Bottom Line for Most Traders

We keep simple records of your trades so tax time is easy. Most small trades result in little or no net tax. We'll send you a clear year-end summary with everything your accountant needs — no digging through emails, no guessing on values.

✅
Good News: It's Often Less Than You Think

If you trade a used couch worth $150 for a used bike worth $150, your tax liability at a 22% bracket is about $33. At a 12% bracket it's about $18. Most people find the actual dollar amount much smaller than the word "taxable" suggested.

🌱

Trading is Good for the Planet

Beyond the financial benefits, every trade on The Swap Yard keeps a usable item out of the waste stream. The Swap Yard is the eco-conscious alternative to buying new — and your trades have real environmental impact regardless of their tax treatment.

🏛
This Applies Everywhere — We Just Make It Easy

Barter income is taxable whether you trade on The Swap Yard, Facebook Marketplace, or in your front yard. The difference is that we help you stay compliant automatically — so you're protected whether you're trading a lamp or a service worth thousands.

Approach 6 — Know Your Exemptions

What Is NOT Taxable on The Swap Yard

Many users will be relieved to find that large categories of trades on our platform create zero or minimal tax liability.

✅ Generally Not Taxable
  • 📉Personal property traded at a loss — if you trade a couch you paid $800 for and the FMV is now $150, you have a loss, not a gain
  • 🎁Gifts received — items given to you as a gift are not income to the recipient
  • 🚗No-fault insurance disability benefits — compensation for lost income under no-fault car insurance
  • 🏥Medical expense reimbursements — generally not taxable though may reduce your medical deduction
  • ♿Permanent injury compensation — compensation based solely on a physical injury or disfigurement
⚠️ Commonly Misunderstood
  • 💰Referral credits — may be taxable when redeemed. Consult your CPA on timing of recognition
  • 🔄Equal-value trades — a $200 for $200 trade still generates $200 of gross income even with zero net gain
  • 💳Platform credits — barter club credits or platform tokens are taxable the moment added to your account per IRS rules
  • 📦Personal property losses — most personal property losses are NOT deductible even when they are not taxable gains
⚠️
This Is General Information, Not Tax Advice

The information on this page is educational and based on published IRS guidance. Your specific situation depends on your filing status, income level, the nature of the items traded, and whether you are operating as a business or individual. Always consult a qualified tax professional for advice specific to your situation.

Approach 7 — Secure Tax Setup via Stripe

Complete Your Tax Setup

The Swap Yard uses Stripe to collect and securely store all required tax information. We never see or store your SSN or EIN — Stripe handles everything directly.

🔒
Your Tax Data Is Held by Stripe — Not Us

When you complete Stripe's onboarding, your W-9 information (including your TIN) is encrypted and stored by Stripe — a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified company. The Swap Yard receives only the last 4 digits of your TIN and a verification status. We never see your raw Social Security Number or EIN.

✅ What Stripe Collects
  • 📋Legal name and business name
  • 🏦Bank account for payouts
  • 🔐SSN or EIN (encrypted by Stripe)
  • 🪪Government ID (for identity verification)
  • 📬Mailing address for tax forms
✅ What The Swap Yard Receives
  • ✔️Verification status (verified / pending)
  • 🔢Last 4 digits of TIN only
  • 🏦Payout account token (not account number)
  • 📧Email address for notifications
💳 Complete Your Tax Setup with Stripe

Takes about 5 minutes. Stripe's secure onboarding collects everything needed for tax compliance and payout setup in one flow. You'll be redirected back to The Swap Yard dashboard when complete.

❓
Why Is This Required?

The IRS requires all barter exchanges and marketplace platforms to collect tax identification from vendors. By delegating this entirely to Stripe, The Swap Yard ensures your sensitive information is handled by a company with the infrastructure and legal expertise to protect it — not stored on our servers.

How It Works — Step by Step

  1. 1

    Click "Complete Tax Setup via Stripe"

    You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure hosted onboarding page — the same system used by Lyft, Instacart, and thousands of other platforms.

  2. 2

    Enter Your Information Directly with Stripe

    Stripe collects your legal name, address, SSN or EIN, and bank account. This information goes directly to Stripe's encrypted systems — The Swap Yard never sees it.

  3. 3

    Stripe Verifies Your Identity

    Stripe may ask you to upload a government ID photo. This is Stripe's standard KYC (Know Your Customer) process required by US financial regulations.

  4. 4

    You're Redirected Back to The Swap Yard

    Once complete, Stripe sends you back to your dashboard with a "Tax Setup Complete" confirmation. You can now accept payments and complete barter trades over $600.

  5. 5

    Stripe Handles 1099-K Reporting Automatically

    For payment transactions, Stripe files your 1099-K with the IRS automatically. For barter transactions, The Swap Yard generates your annual barter report using only the non-sensitive trade data we collect.
