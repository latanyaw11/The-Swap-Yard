🔐 Stripe Setup — Required to Accept Card Payments

To accept card payments through The Swap Yard, connect your Stripe account. Stripe securely collects your bank details and W-9 tax info — we never see your SSN or EIN. Only required if you want to accept card payments.

Takes ~5 min · Bank-level security · Skip if using cash/Venmo/Zelle only
