Section 04

Safety Guidelines

⚠️
The Swap Yard Is Not Responsible for In-Person Meetings All in-person meetings, exchanges, and transactions occur entirely at the users' own risk. The Swap Yard strongly encourages all users to follow the safety guidelines below.
📍

Meet in Public

Always meet in a well-lit, busy public place. Police station parking lots, bank lobbies, and coffee shops are ideal.

👥

Bring Someone

Never meet a stranger alone for high-value transactions. Bring a friend or family member with you.

📱

Tell Someone

Always tell a trusted person where you're going, who you're meeting, and when you expect to be back.

🔍

Verify Identity

Check the user's The Swap Yard profile, reviews, and verification badges before meeting. Trust your instincts.

💰

Inspect Before Paying

Always inspect the item before completing payment. Never pay in advance for in-person pickup items.

🚗

Drive Yourself

Use your own transportation. Avoid being dependent on a stranger for a ride to or from the meeting location.

📸

Document Everything

Take photos of the item and the transaction. Keep all communication on the The Swap Yard platform for your records.

🚨

Trust Your Gut

If something feels wrong, leave. No deal is worth your safety. Report suspicious activity to The Swap Yard and local police.

Safe Transaction Zones

Many police departments across the US offer designated "Safe Transaction Zones" — marked parking spots with surveillance cameras specifically for meetups from online marketplaces. Search "safe transaction zone [your city]" to find one near you.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you encounter a listing that appears fraudulent, illegal, or suspicious, use the Report button on any listing immediately. The Swap Yard reviews all reports within 24 hours. For immediate safety concerns, contact local law enforcement first.

Section 05

In-Person Meeting Best Practices

Before the Meeting

  1. Communicate only through The Swap Yard's messaging system until you're comfortable
  2. Agree on a specific time, date, and public location in advance
  3. Verify the item photos match what you expect — request additional photos if needed
  4. Research the fair market value of the item independently
  5. Check the seller's reviews, rating, and account age
  6. Share your meeting details with a trusted friend or family member
  7. Charge your phone fully before leaving

At the Meeting

  1. Meet in a public place with other people present — never at a private residence for first meetings
  2. Arrive in your own vehicle and park in a visible, well-lit area
  3. Thoroughly inspect the item before agreeing to complete the transaction
  4. Test electronics, run vehicles, verify functionality before paying
  5. Count cash carefully or confirm payment processing before handing over the item
  6. If anything feels wrong, you are entitled to leave without completing the transaction

High-Value Transactions ($500+)

For transactions over $500 we strongly recommend:

  • Meeting at a police station lobby or other official Safe Transaction Zone
  • Using The Swap Yard's Stripe payment system rather than cash for a paper trail
  • Bringing a witness
  • Requesting proof of ownership (original receipts, serial number verification)

Barter & Trade Policy

6.1 Barter at Your Own Risk

The Swap Yard facilitates barter and trade exchanges as a core platform feature. However, all barter transactions are conducted entirely between users at their sole risk. The Swap Yard is not a party to any barter agreement and assumes no responsibility for the outcome of trades.

⚠️
Barter Is Not Covered by Buyer Protection The Swap Yard's buyer protection and dispute resolution services apply only to cash and card transactions processed through the platform. Pure barter/trade exchanges are not eligible for dispute resolution or refunds.

6.2 Tax Obligations for Barter

Under IRS regulations, the fair market value of goods or services received in a barter exchange is taxable income. If The Swap Yard facilitates sufficient barter volume for a user, we may be required to issue a 1099-B form. Users are solely responsible for reporting barter income on their tax returns. Consult a qualified tax professional.

6.3 Barter Best Practices

  • Agree on the fair market value of both items in writing before the trade
  • Document the condition of both items with photos before the exchange
  • Use The Swap Yard's messaging system to create a written record of the agreement
  • Consider using The Swap Yard's Trade Confirmation feature to acknowledge mutual agreement
  • For high-value trades, consider having items professionally appraised in advance

6.4 Service Barter

When bartering services (e.g., web design in exchange for photography), both parties should agree on the scope of work, timeline, and deliverables in writing before beginning. The Swap Yard recommends using the platform's messaging system to document all agreements.

♻️ Our Commitment

Sustainability & Environmental Commitment

The Swap Yard is built on the premise that the most sustainable product is one that already exists. By facilitating the resale, barter, and trade of goods between individuals and businesses, The Swap Yard actively promotes circular economy principles and helps divert usable items from landfills.

12.1 Our Platform's Environmental Position

The Swap Yard explicitly positions itself as an eco-conscious alternative to buying new. Every transaction on our platform — whether a sale, trade, or barter — represents a good that found a second life rather than entering the waste stream. We believe that marketplace platforms have a responsibility to support sustainable consumption, and we have built that belief into our platform's design, messaging, and community guidelines.

12.2 What We Encourage

  • Listing items you no longer need rather than discarding them
  • Buying secondhand before buying new
  • Repairing and reselling items rather than replacing them
  • Trading services to reduce cash consumption and build community
  • Estate sale and yard sale listings to rehome household goods
  • Posting items with honest condition descriptions to maximize reuse potential

12.3 Platform Operations

The Swap Yard is a software platform hosted on Netlify's global edge network with green energy commitments. Our backend database is hosted on Supabase, which operates on energy-efficient cloud infrastructure. We do not operate physical locations, warehouses, or delivery fleets, which significantly reduces our direct carbon footprint relative to traditional retail or logistics companies.

12.4 Community Standards

We ask all Swap Yard community members to uphold the spirit of sustainable commerce. This includes accurately representing item conditions, packaging shipments responsibly using recycled materials where possible, and prioritizing local pickup to eliminate shipping emissions when practical.
